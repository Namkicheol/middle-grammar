Created 2026-09-12 with the built-in GPT image generation tool for this project. No external game artwork was copied. Commercial release still needs a final asset and rights review; generation alone is not legal clearance.

## Production files

- `night-classroom.png`: rear-facing classroom, chalkboard, teacher drawer, cabinet, right exit door.
- `night-archive.png`: archive desk, three books, brass compartment, clock, right exit door.
- `night-corridor.png`: intersecting school hallway, moonlit windows, distant amber exit.
- `explorer.png`: transparent top-down explorer sprite with teal jacket, amber backpack and flashlight.

## Generation briefs

Shared: original teenage horror escape game production background, wide 16:9, abandoned Korean school at night, premium cinematic stylized realism, midnight teal moonlight and restrained amber practical light, worn wood/plaster, subtle dust and mist. No people, monsters, gore, UI, legible text, logos or watermark. Props must remain distinguishable at tablet size.

Classroom: view from rear toward chalkboard and right closed door; teacher desk with brass key drawer lower middle, cabinet left, moonlight through left windows. Dark outer lower corners for UI.

Archive: use classroom only as style reference, not edit target. Distinct archive/library room with tall shelves, center librarian desk with muted red/teal/ochre books and locked brass compartment, upper-left analog clock, far-right door.

Corridor: use classroom only as style reference. Long eerie hallway intersecting into blue darkness, closed doors right, moonlit windows left, worn reflective floor, amber far exit. Background art rather than overhead maze map.

Explorer: single top-down teenage explorer, dark teal jacket, worn amber backpack, upward flashlight, premium painterly stylized realism and clear small-size silhouette. True transparent background; no environment, floor, cast floor shadow, text, logos, weapons, gore, or sprite sheet.

Original generated files remain in the Codex generated-images directory. Runtime assets are copied here so the game does not depend on that directory.
