# Solo race artwork

- `kart-blue-atlas-v1.png`: original OpenAI image generation, 2026-10-27; straight, left and right chase views of the same blue kart, generated from an original concept image for this project. The atlas is used for the blue SPRINT player; other existing kart choices retain their existing assets.
- `hill-panorama-v1.jpg`: original OpenAI image generation, 2026-10-27; distant landscape without a painted road. Converted from the generated PNG to JPEG quality 78 for browser delivery.
- Neither image was copied from a third-party game. Existing artwork remains documented in `SOURCES.md` and `coastal-v3/SOURCES.md`.
- `kart-fox-v1.webp`, `kart-robot-v1.webp`, `kart-owl-v1.webp`: original OpenAI image generation, 2026-10-27, for this project. Rear and left-steering frames are rendered directly; the left-steering frame is mirrored for the opposite direction to maintain matching geometry.
- `kart-raccoon-v1.webp`, `kart-digger-v1.webp`, `kart-penguin-v1.webp`, `kart-frog-v1.webp`, `kart-rabbit-v1.webp`: original OpenAI image generation, 2026-10-27. Each atlas has three chase views; one turn view is mirrored for the opposite direction. Frog's central cell is its straight view.

Character atlases are lossless WebP conversions of the generated transparent PNGs.
